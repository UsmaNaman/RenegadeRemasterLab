# Legal stance

This is a statement of project policy, not legal advice. Laws differ between countries;
check what applies to you.

- **You supply your own dumps.** RRL works only on images of games and hardware you legally
  own and dumped yourself. It never modifies your original files.
- **RRL ships nothing copyrighted.** No game images, executables, extracted assets, keys,
  BIOS, MCPX or other firmware dumps, in the code, releases, test fixtures or documentation.
- **No links** to sites that distribute ROMs, disc images, BIOS files or keys.
- **No DRM circumvention.** RRL does not include, and will not accept, code that breaks
  encryption or copy protection.
- **No hacking guides.** No softmod, JTAG, RGH or similar console modification instructions.
- **Piracy requests are closed.** Issues, discussions or pull requests that ask for, share or
  point to copyrighted data are closed and the content removed. Repeat offenders are blocked.

Contributors certify their work with the DCO (see [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)).
